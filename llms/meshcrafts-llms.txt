# Meshcrafts (CURRENT)

> Meshcrafts, operating publicly as CURRENT (Current Eco AS, formerly Meshcrafts AS, of Oslo, Norway), is a hardware-agnostic charge point management system (CPMS) for electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure. The platform lets location owners and charge point operators manage charging points, handle payment and invoicing, apply smart energy management and load balancing, and offer white-label charging to drivers. It is OCPP-certified for OCPP 1.6 and OCPP 2.0.1 (including Advanced Security) and advertises 99% uptime. Backed by 500 Global.
