Since Åsmund Møll Frengstad founded CURRENT in 2014, we have played a crucial role in developing the industry standard, OCPP. As a result, it is natural for us to be among the first globally to receive certification when a new OCPP version is launched. Today, we are proud to announce that we are the first management system in the Nordic region to achieve both OCPP 2.0.1 CORE and Advanced Security certifications.
