DEFA, a leader in EV charging technology, has extensive experience in delivering OCPP 2.0.1 as a standard for its 22 kW AC charger, DEFA Power. This next-generation solution ensures seamless integration with existing and future charging infrastructure while elevating the user experience for EV owners. In collaboration with CURRENT, a leading independent Charge System Management Systems (CSMS) provider in the Nordics, DEFA offers user-friendly and secure ad hoc payment solutions compliant with the European Union’s AFIR directive (Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation).
